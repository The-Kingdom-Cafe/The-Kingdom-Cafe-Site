The Craft

Small kitchen.
Royal treatment.

The Kingdom Cafe was built on a simple idea — that a two-rupee bite of chaat deserves the same care as a fine-dining plate. Everything is sourced, prepped, and finished in-house, and every plate is built to order. Nothing sits.

— I —

Waters pressed each morning

Mint, tamarind, jaljeera and chilli — four waters cold-steeped daily. When they're done, they're done.

— II —

Plated to order

Puris stay sealed until you order. Pav toasts on butter while your bhaji finishes on the tawa. No heat lamps.

— III —

A fair crown

Pani puri starts at ₹25. Chaat at ₹40. We refuse to price ourselves out of the neighbourhood that raised us.

Our Signature

Four waters.
One crown.

Every Kingdom pani puri comes with a choice of four waters, hand-tuned each morning. Order any single flavour, or ask for the flight and try all four. This is the dish we're known for.

Mint & Coriander

Pudina Pani

Cold-steeped mint, coriander, green chilli, black salt. Bright and cooling.

Sweet Tamarind

Meetha Pani

Slow-reduced tamarind and jaggery with roasted cumin. Deep, dark, sweet-sour.

Jaljeera

Zeera Pani

Roasted cumin, dry mango and mint. The classic North Indian summer cooler.
